## Trust Boundaries

|----------|-------------|
| 飞书 → webhook/WS 入口 | 未签名/伪造事件可触发同步 |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-83-02-SPOOF | Spoofing | drive 事件入口 | mitigate | 现有飞书签名校验（FEISHU_SIGNATURE_REQUIRED）+ ProcessedEvent 幂等 + durable idempotency_key |
| T-83-02-INFO | Information Disclosure | pull 日志/留痕 | mitigate | 正文/异常 redact_secrets_in_text；原始 payload record_inbound_webhook 脱敏；token 不入日志 |
| T-83-02-DOS | Denial of Service | 高频编辑刷事件 | mitigate | per-doc lock=docsync-{file_token} 串行 + idempotency 去重；per-block sampling+debug |

| T-83-02-FAILSOFT | Tampering | pull 失败反噬 | mitigate | best-effort，失败置 broken，绝不抛回 webhook/编辑主流程 |
</threat_model>
