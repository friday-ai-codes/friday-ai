| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-88-04-INFO | Information Disclosure | 卡片 action_value 携正文 | mitigate | action_value 仅携 execution_id/node_id/round/action，正文走流式灌入（mirror board_split） |
