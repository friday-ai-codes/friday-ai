## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-41-03-01 | Tampering | PlanSession/canonical 写入 | mitigate | 节点不旁路写表：session 经 PlanSessionService、canonical 经 TechnicalPlanService（adapter 内）、Clarification 经 ClarificationService（INV-6）；engine 只经 transition 改 status |
| T-41-03-02 | Denial of Service | engine.advance 驱动循环 | mitigate | 驱动循环设最大步数上限 + 挂起条件 break；advance 不前进/异常 → fail 终态，避免节点无限占用 |
